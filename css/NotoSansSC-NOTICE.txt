NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf

Noto Sans SC Regular, version 2.004, converted from its OpenType (CFF)
outlines to TrueType outlines so that pdf-lib can embed only the characters
a document uses. Its glyphs, metrics and names are otherwise unchanged; the
vertical layout and OpenType layout tables are not carried.
