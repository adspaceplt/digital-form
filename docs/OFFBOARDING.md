# Offboarding

What to do in the portal when a colleague leaves, or when a client's contact
or the client itself leaves. Each step names where it is done. Nothing here
deletes a colleague: a colleague is stood down and the record stays, because
every task, letter and decision they made names them.

## A colleague leaves

Do these in order, on or before the last day.

| # | Step | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the last day as Access until (a day, and a time if access ends during it). Access ends by itself at that moment, every five minutes checked. | Team › the colleague's ⋯ › Edit › Sign-in and access |
| 2 | Move their open tasks to whoever takes them. | My Work › The whole team › Group by assignee › ⋯ › Select tasks › Reassign |
| 3 | Reports waiting for them to confirm go to another reviewer. | Reports › In review › the report › ⋯ › Change reviewer |
| 4 | Turn their namecard off. Its link and printed QR show the closed card until it is turned on again. | Team › Edit › Namecard › Card Off |
| 5 | Close their month in Performance: finish or release the last review, and decide any initiative they proposed. | Team › Performance |
| 6 | On the last day, or once access has ended: Set inactive. The portal asks who takes their clients and open campaigns; Keep is offered first. | Team › the colleague's ⋯ › Set inactive |
| 7 | Issue any HR letter the departure needs. Shared, it stays readable to them only while they can sign in. | Documents › Issue document › HR letters |

Once inactive, or past Access until, every request they make is refused by
the database: no page, link or session they still hold reads or writes
anything. Their notifications stop with it.

### Outside the portal

The portal does not hold these. Each is closed by its owner the same day:

- the Google Workspace account and any shared calendar access;
- Meta Business Suite and the ad accounts;
- the WhatsApp Business account, if they were an admin of it;
- any AWS, Supabase, GitHub or Cloudflare access they were given.

## A client's contact leaves

| # | Step | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revoke their portal access. Undo stays in place for a moment. | Clients › the record › Contacts › the contact's ⋯ › Revoke portal access |
| 2 | If they were the main contact, make another contact the main one, and change the billing contact if it was them. | Contacts › the other contact's ⋯ › Main contact; Billing › Edit |
| 3 | If they held the review or script link, reset it and send the new one to whoever takes over. The old link stops at once. A campaign's selection link has no reset; it closes with the selection. | Content Review › the client's ⋯ › Reset access link; Video Scripts › the month's ⋯ › Reset client link |
| 4 | Remove the contact. It is kept, hidden, and can be restored. | Contacts › ⋯ › Remove |
| 5 | Only where the person asks for their data to be erased (PDPA 2010, PDPA 2012 in Singapore): Delete. Records that named them keep their own words. | Contacts › Show removed contacts › the contact's ⋯ › Delete (Clients: Contacts at Full Access) |

## A client leaves

Move the client to Past from the stage select. The portal asks why (Budget,
Results, Moved in-house, Business closed, No reply, Other) and counts the
client's open tasks; each owner is told once, and those tasks read Urgent
delivery in My Work until they are finished or the client returns to Active.

Then, as each applies:

- revoke every contact's portal access (above);
- Remove from Content Review keeps every set and approval and closes the link;
- reset the script link if it should stop working;
- finish or cancel the months still open in My Work (a month's ⋯ › Cancel month).

Deleting a client is for a record that should not exist (keyed twice, a
test). Past is how a client leaves.
